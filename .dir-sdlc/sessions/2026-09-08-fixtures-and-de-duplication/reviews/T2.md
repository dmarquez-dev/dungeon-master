## `contradicts` #1 — criterion 3's verification command is unsatisfiable given the shape criterion 3 itself mandates

**Falsifies:** `manifest.json:55`.

The criterion requires a two-level hierarchy and then asks that
`grep -ln ": CommandHandlerTestBase" Commands/*.cs | wc -l` return 14. **C# permits one base class**, so
the twelve not-found leaves textually name `DungeonNotFoundHandlerTestBase<T>` and never
`CommandHandlerTestBase`. Reviewer measured that grep at **2**; T2's union form at **14**.

**The reviewer confirmed T2's load-bearing premise independently:** `CreateDungeonCommandHandler.cs:28`
commits directly with no `LoadAsync`, so `CreateDungeon` has **no not-found path**, and a flat 14-way
hierarchy would assert not-found behaviour on a handler that cannot produce it. *The literal grep can
return 14 only under a shape the same criterion forbids.*

- **Option A (best): accept T2's substituted measurement and record the criterion as defective.** The
  shape clause and the grep clause cannot both hold; the shape clause carries the design intent and is
  the one the definition of done names. *(`repo` — `manifest.json:55` mandates the two-level shape in
  its own prose; `CreateDungeonCommandHandler.cs:28` shows the flat alternative would be wrong)*
- **Option B: amend the criterion's grep to the union form for future sessions**, so a criterion
  checking "all N share a root base" survives an intermediate base. *(`judgment`)*
- **Option C: keep the criterion and flatten the hierarchy to satisfy it literally.** Not recommended —
  it would put `HandleAsync_WithMissingDungeon_…` on `CreateDungeon`, which has no such path.
  *(`repo` — `CreateDungeonCommandHandler.cs:28`)*
