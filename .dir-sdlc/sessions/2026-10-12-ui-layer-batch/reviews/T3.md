## A new instrument hazard, found by the reviewer in its OWN work

Worth more than the advisories above. The reviewer's first type-uniqueness predicate,
`(record|class|struct|enum)[[:space:]]+<T>[^A-Za-z0-9_]`, returned **0** for `ObjectKind`, `ObjectRotation`
and `ObjectScale` — **not an absence**, but the trailing `[^A-Za-z0-9_]` having nothing left to match once
`grep -E` stripped the CR on a line that **ends at the type name**. Corrected to `([^A-Za-z0-9_]|$)`, all five
types return 1.

**This is the mirror of the known `[^.]`-matches-the-CR trap**: there the character class *consumes* the CR
and matches wrongly; here it *requires* a character the CR-stripping removed and fails to match at all. Both
are silent. Recorded in `measurement.md` as an addition to the instrument register.

The reviewer also credited the implementer's own disclosed near-miss — `grep -c '[^ -~]'` returning non-zero
and *looking like* confirmation of criterion 9, the matches actually being tab lines — as the same class of
failure, caught by its author.
