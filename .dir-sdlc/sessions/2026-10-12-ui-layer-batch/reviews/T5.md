## Advisory findings

**A1 — the implementer's own naive route 2 is structurally inert, so its fixture zero was vacuous.** The
reviewer **read the source** rather than accepting the output: `naive_routes.py:route2` appends a hit only
when `tail.find("{") == -1`, i.e. only when no brace exists **anywhere later in the file**, so it returns 0
on essentially any input. Criterion 8's literal requirement is still met — the real detector returns 1 on the
braceless twin and not the braced one, and route 1 **is** genuinely discriminating — so this is not blocking.
**The implementer disclosed this itself and declined to claim the orchestrator's attributed figure of 32.
The reviewer could not reproduce that 32 either; it remains attributed and unreproduced.** Recorded as
CORRECTION 5 in `measurement.md`.

**A2 — criterion 12's literal `case ` predicate and the reported 15 are different populations, both equal
before and after.** Unanchored `grep -c 'case '` returns **19 → 19**, four matches being prose in comments;
the anchored `^[[:space:]]*case ` returns **15 → 15**, the true switch-label count. The criterion's
requirement — equality before and after — **holds on both predicates**, so this is a reporting note so Gate 3
does not read "15" against the criterion's `case ` wording as a discrepancy.

**A3 — criteria 1 and 15 are discharged by report statements the reviewer was not handed.** The briefing
summarised the report extensively but omitted the per-edit tool ledger criterion 1 names and the explicit
"I ran neither command" of criterion 15. The reviewer marked both met **because the substantive property of
criterion 1 is independently verified by stronger evidence than the assertion itself** — 100% CRLF intact
across all three files positively excludes both forbidden writers — and asked the orchestrator to confirm the
statements exist. **ORCHESTRATOR CONFIRMATION: they do, verbatim.** T5's report states "every write to the
three source files was the `Edit` tool — 20 Edit calls (15 / 3 / 2) … I used no `sed -i` and no `Write` on
any of the three", and "NO BUILD, NO TEST, NO GIT (criterion 15). I ran no `dotnet build`, no `dotnet test`,
and no `git` command." **This is the orchestrator's third instance of relaying a summary where the report was
required** — recorded as CORRECTION 6 in `measurement.md`, and practice changed for wave 2.

**A4 — ownership is clean as far as the diff can show.** T5's changes are confined to its three owned paths,
all brace-only. `ObjectPaletteView.xaml.cs` is changed in the worktree but its diff is `42/0` — 40 lines of
