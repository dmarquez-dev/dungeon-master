## What the reviewer verified beyond the criteria

**No contract violation.** `git status` touches exactly the six owned globs. `AppShell.xaml.cs` — used
for a probe and **not in T3's owns set** — is blob-identical to `b3b1641` (`83c391d4…`).
`grep -rn "DmProbe\|DM_PROBE" src/` returns nothing.

**`BackgroundColor="Transparent"` is sound, with no side effect found.** WinUI genuinely does not
hit-test a null `Background` and a MAUI layout panel has none, so without it the recogniser would sit
on a surface the pointer passes through and **the fix would be inert while looking applied**.
Transparent paints nothing, so no pixels and no layout change. The one behavioural consequence — the
container is hit-testable across its whole extent, including below the last card — is deliberate,
covered by `ADropBeyondTheListResolvesToTheNearestCard`, and bounded because a `VerticalStackLayout`
sizes to its content. The container carries no tap gesture, so nothing competes with the cards.

**Band geometry checks out at the production seam, not only in the fixture.** `FloorSidebarItem` is a
`ContentView` whose content is `Border x:Name="FloorBorder"` with `Margin="0,0,0,4"`, and that Border
carries the `DropGestureRecognizer` — so `DropTargetWithin` returns the Border and the band height is
its **54**, not the ContentView's 58. A band built from the child would have swallowed the 4-DIU seam
exactly as T3 said. The reviewer re-derived all 17 fixture cases by hand against `CardPitch=58` /
`CardHeight=54`; all arithmetically correct, including the two ties.

**Three `ReorderFloorCommand` call sites, counted by the reviewer**: `DungeonDetailPage.xaml.cs:860`,
`:2298` (new), `:2418` (the placeholder closure). Exactly T3's numbers. A fourth hit at
`FloorListViewModel.cs:190` is the command's **construction**, not a call site.

## Advisory findings

1. **`ItemBorder` now hosts two `PointerGestureRecognizer`s** — one from `HoverOrLongPressBehavior`
   (`:79-82`), one from `HandCursorBehavior` (`:24-27`) — the only place in the two controls where they
   share a host. The observation confirmed the cursor but not that the hover highlight still fires.
   **Reviewer's ruling when asked whether this should be blocking: no.** The other recogniser was
   already there and T3 did not touch it, MAUI dispatches to every recogniser rather than stopping at
   the first, and no acceptance criterion covers the area row's hover highlight — without a criterion
   it cannot be blocking, and there is no evidence of a regression, only an unobserved interaction.
   **Folded into the next observation pass.**
2. **Truncated floor names have no recovery affordance.** `LineBreakMode="TailTruncation"` +
   `MaxLines="1"` (`FloorSidebarItem.xaml:168-169`) is well justified — it keeps C3 §2 true for a name
   of *any* length rather than for the eleven in the database — and the cost was disclosed rather than
   hidden. But "Main Tave…" cannot be read in full, and the file's own `Button`s already use
   `ToolTipProperties.Text`. **To the backlog at Gate 3.**

   `Tests/App/Layout/`, because `Tests/AppCore/FloorReorder*.cs` is **T1's** glob and was not granted.
