## Option A vs Option B -- ruled, not deferred to a revision round

The renamed host test still executes zero assertions, because its collection is always empty today. The implementer took **Option A** (rename plus `DisplayName`) and offered **Option B** (`Assert.Empty(handled)`), declining B because it could measure the recorder empty only on this machine today.

**The reviewer ruled Option A satisfies criteria 6 and 7 as written**, and that it is what §4.2 actually prescribes:

- Criterion 6 asks for a rename stating only what the test establishes -- done, and the `DisplayName` puts the inertness into the test **report**, which is §4.1's remedy applied to the sibling case and strictly more than criterion 6 demands.
- Criterion 7 forbids an `Assert.NotEmpty` on `HandledExceptions` and forbids anything resting on the recorder firing. A does neither.
- Option B is required by neither criterion, and the objection to it is sound: `Assert.Empty(handled)` asserts the recorder is **empty**, which is a claim about the health of a particular run rather than about the fixture's contract, and it goes red on the first genuinely recorded exception on a healthier host.

**Not worth a revision round.** The A/B choice is a Gate 3 matter, not a finding.

## Advisory finding

**The second guard at `:238` cannot fail while `:230` stands.** `Assert.Equal(ShippedPackKinds.Count * 2, Decoded.Count)` at 230 means that if `Decoded.Count` is `2N`, then `Skip(N)` always yields `N` items, so `Assert.NotEmpty(refilled)` at 238 can never fire. It is correct, in scope and harmless documentation of intent, but it is **protection against a future weakening of line 230 rather than protection that bites today**. No criterion rests on it.
