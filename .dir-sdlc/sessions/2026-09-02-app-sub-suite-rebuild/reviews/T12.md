## The move is a pure insertion -- confirmed independently

`git diff --numstat 3328b05 -- ThemeSource.cs` -> **`185 0`**, and a count of removed lines -> **0**.
The four hunks (`+74,5`, `+150,15`, `+539,126`, `+748,39`) show the members were **interleaved into
`ThemeSource`'s existing ordering** — consts with consts, regexes with regexes, publics, then private
helpers — rather than appended as a block. That is a reordering of the moved lines among themselves,
not an edit to any of them.

A multiset compare of the old class body (170 non-blank lines) against the 185 inserted (170
non-blank): **162 identical, 8 only-old / 8 only-new, every one inside one `<summary>`.** T12's
figures are exact.

## The one disclosed doc-comment edit -- correct, necessary, correctly disclosed

The reviewer's own word-level `difflib` returns **exactly one substantive opcode pair**:
`delete ['left']` and `replace ['file'] -> ['reference', 'scan']` — *"the one regular expression **left
in this file**"* becoming *"the one regular expression **in this reference scan**"*. Everything else is
reflow that changed no other word.

**Necessary, not tidy:** kept verbatim the sentence becomes flatly false. `ThemeSource.cs` declares
six regexes and `ThemeTokenParityTests.cs` now declares none, so "the one regex left in this file"
would assert the opposite of the truth *in the file the reader is standing in*. Both protected halves
survive: the **uniqueness** claim (verified — `StaticResourceKeys` still does zero regex work,
hand-parsing the extension) and the **boundary** claim, word for word. This is criterion 3's
"cross-reference paths" allowance applied to a claim whose referent moved.

## Advisory findings

**A1 -- the `MASTER-138` two-private-copies note was dropped rather than rehomed.** Its surrounding
E9 rationale is correctly gone, since this task discharged it, and none of it is among §3.6's three
named comments — so this is **not** a criterion violation. The convention itself survives, cited at
four other sites. **What is lost is the record of *why these five members are shared surface rather
than duplicated*, which is precisely the thing a future reader is most likely to undo.** One sentence
on `ThemeSource`'s class summary restores it. **Do not send T12 back** — fold it into whichever task
next writes to `ThemeSource.cs`.

**A2 -- `StaticResourceKeys` has zero external call sites, before and after.** Confirmed
independently: two references only, the declaration at `:592` and `.SelectMany(StaticResourceKeys)`
at `:555` inside `References()` in the same class. It could be `private` on today's evidence, yet
§3.6 declares it public surface. **T12 was right not to touch it** — criterion 7 forbids exactly
that. Its two companions confirmed the same way: `ThemeFileText` **zero** external references,
`KeyDefinitionPattern` **one** (`ThemeSourceTests.cs:458`, reachable only as `ThemeSource` ⏎
`.KeyDefinitionPattern`). **Three declared-public members with 0/0/1 external callers is a
contract-surface question, and the right unit of decision is all three at once.**

**A3 -- criterion 2's prescribed command is defective, and the observation is sharper than the
defect.** `grep -nE "^\tpublic static"` returns nothing, exit 1, because GNU ERE reads `\t` as a
literal `t`; run against both the before-body and the inserted block, empty twice. The
`[[:space:]]+` form returns 5 over the class body and **25** over today's file.

> **Criterion 10 of this very task forbids `grep -c '^\t'` in evidence, while criterion 2 mandates
> the same construct eight criteria earlier. The prohibition and the defect are in one manifest
> entry.**

The session's **fifth** grep-form defect and the **second time this exact form was written into a
criterion after being adjudicated**. A criterion-authoring defect, not a T12 defect; T12 found it,
reported it, and substituted a working form. **It warrants a standing manifest lint rather than
another adjudication.**

**A4 -- criterion 5's call-site figures are wrong.** It claims "4 call sites in each file… 8". Measured
at the base commit: **4** in `ThemeDictionaryTests.cs`, **6** in `ThemeTokenParityTests.cs`, **10**
lines = 1 declaration + 2 crefs + **7** call sites (4 parity, 3 dictionary). Not 8, and not
symmetric. The criterion's own escape hatch — "the task re-derives that figure and accounts for any
difference" — is what makes it met, and T12 used it correctly.

**A5 -- criterion 8's prescribed evidence cannot prove its claim.** `git log --oneline -1` shows one
commit; it cannot show that five named tasks are all committed. The claim is true, but the command
named is not what establishes it. `git log --oneline -12`, or a per-task `--grep`, would be. **Same
class as A3 and A4.**
