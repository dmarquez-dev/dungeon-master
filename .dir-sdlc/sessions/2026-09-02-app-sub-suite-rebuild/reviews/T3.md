## Advisory findings

**A1 -- criterion 14's `git diff --name-only` clause is defective, and the same clause recurs in
T4:9, T8:13 and T9:12.** Three of T3's four deliverables are untracked, so `git diff` cannot list
them; and the shared worktree makes the command report five files that are not T3's. Measured:
`git status --porcelain` lists 6 modified and 6 untracked entries spanning T1, T2 and T3. The
criterion prescribes evidence the tool does not print. T8 and T9 also create new files, so they
inherit the defect.

Options, as the reviewer worded them:
1. *(repo)* Replace with `git status --porcelain -- <the task's owns globs>`, which lists tracked and
   untracked alike and scopes away peer work. This is the form every task in this session can
   actually run. Evidence: `git status --porcelain` verified to enumerate untracked deliverables that
   `git diff --name-only` omits entirely.
2. *(judgment)* Keep `git diff --name-only` but pair it with `git ls-files --others --exclude-standard -- <owns>`.
   Preserves the wording at the cost of two commands.

**A2 -- criterion 8 and contract §3.3 cite the wrong line for `GeometryScaleTests`'s comment
patterns.** Measured: `XmlCommentPattern` at `:838`, `WholeLineCommentPattern` at `:845`. The cited
`:720-723` and `:727-730` are inside a method body. Independently confirms the orchestrator's own
check. **T3 itself is unaffected** -- its doc comment cites the pattern **by name**, which is the
better form and worth propagating.

**A3 -- `public static string QualifiedElementName(XName)` is additive, outside contract §3, and the
addition is sound.** Criterion 6 requires the qualification be asserted against a synthetic document;
§3 exposes the value only as a property over the real tree, offering no injection point. The method
is the minimum surface that makes the criterion satisfiable, and criterion 5 forbids *removals* from
§1/§2, not additions. Flagging rather than escalating was right given five dependent tasks. The
contract question is the orchestrator's to escalate.

**A4 -- `anti-vacuity.md` §1.1's "full inventory" of fourteen sites across eight files is itself
incomplete: there are at least sixteen across nine.** Two further helper assertions in a ninth file
the table does not list: `DmThemeKeysResolveTests.cs:195` in `ResolveCalls()` (`:178`) and `:222` in
`KeyedElementsInThemeDictionaries()` (`:201`). Both are already implicitly handled -- T9 c.5
preserves the first, T9 c.8 rewrites the second's helper -- so nothing is at risk, but §1.1 states
"every one of the fourteen is retired" and the figure is wrong.

Options, as the reviewer worded them:
1. *(repo)* Amend §1.1 to sixteen across nine, adding the two `DmThemeKeysResolveTests` rows against
