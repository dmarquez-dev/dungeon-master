**A2 -- criterion 7's "640 baseline" is stale, and the criterion as literally worded cannot be
satisfied.** 665 − 6 = 659, not 640; closing it requires a **third** term, T3's 19, committed after
T2's `startRef`. The implementer cannot make its two numbers account for the 640 difference without
naming a peer's work. This is a finding about the frozen criterion, not about the task.

**A3 -- the two `AppSourceFiles` facts run against the real checkout.** Unavoidable: that is the
surface under test, and they depend on the checkout being *valid*, not on where it sits on disk --
injection B reddened them precisely because root resolution broke, which is correct behaviour.
Recorded only because criterion 3's temp-directory requirement makes the contrast worth noting; it
applies to the three layout facts, and those are all constructed.
