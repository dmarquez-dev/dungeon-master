## The vacuity finding, independently confirmed

The implementer reported that `ConstructInflatesTheApplicationShell` stayed **green** under two
separate injections -- a bare `Microsoft.Maui.Controls.Application` in place of
`DungeonMaster.App.App` (removing the theme dictionaries) and `DispatcherProvider.SetCurrent(null)`
-- because `AppShell.xaml` declares no `StaticResource` and attaches no dispatcher-dependent
behaviour. A bare non-null check survived a fixture supplying **neither** of its two prerequisites.
The implementer strengthened the fact to also assert `shell.Items` is non-empty.

**The reviewer confirmed this with a third, different injection**: removing `InitializeComponent()`
from `AppShell.xaml.cs` turns the fact red **on `shell.Items`**, exactly where a bare non-null check
would have survived. Two independent injections agreeing is much stronger than either alone.

This is the enumeration-and-vacuity family the session was convened to eliminate, found inside the
session's own brand-new fixture, on the first task, by running a check rather than reading one.

## Advisory findings

**A1 -- criterion 8's "any additional member is internal" cannot be satisfied literally.** The frozen
surface in `app-unit-tests-project.md` §4 declares `AppFixture : IDisposable`, and the type is an
xunit `ICollectionFixture`, so a public parameterless constructor and a public `Dispose()` are both
mandatory and both are "additional members" that cannot be internal (`AppFixture.cs:40` and `:119`).
The implementation is correct; the criterion is over-strict. No revision warranted. A future session
should word it as "any additional member **beyond those the frozen declaration forces**".

**A2 -- criterion 14 as worded contradicts `build-file-ownership.md` §5 for the lock files.** It says
"every file this task creates or edits is tab-indented and CRLF"; the two `packages.lock.json` files
are generated, space-indented per `.editorconfig`'s `[*.{json,yml,yaml}]`, and §5 says explicitly
"commit what restore writes; do not hand-edit". Judged met on the contract-consistent reading -- CRLF
does hold, 1356/1356 and 506/506 -- but the criterion should be scoped to hand-authored files.

**A3 -- criterion 5's `grep -n "_ ="` is strictly broader than the guard it stands in for.**
`DiscardedTasksAreAbsentTests` tests the trimmed *line start*, so a `///` line quoting the token
passes the test while failing the criterion's grep. That is exactly the disagreement the implementer
hit and resolved in the criterion's favour. The current file satisfies both, and the reviewer
confirmed both gates are live by injection, so criterion 5 is genuinely met. Recorded so the next
author knows the criterion is the stricter of the two gates.

**A4 -- criterion 12's `640` is stale on this branch and will read as a failure to anyone running it
cold.** The literal command prints 665 here. Not a T1 defect -- the delta is fully attributed to
uncommitted T2 and T3 work by the filtered count above.
