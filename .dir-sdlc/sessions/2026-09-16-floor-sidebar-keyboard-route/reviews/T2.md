## The §1.7 survival claim — independently confirmed, and correctly scoped

This is the finding the disposition of `MASTER-098` rests on, so it was checked three times by three parties
rather than inherited.

The reviewer ran `grep -rn "IsExpanded\|IsSelected" src/DungeonMaster.App --include=*.xaml --include=*.cs`
with `obj/`, `bin/` and both declaring files excluded: **exactly seven hits.** Three page-side writes at
`DungeonDetailPage.xaml.cs:832`, `:965`, `:2204`; three `IsVisible="{Binding IsExpanded, Source={x:Reference
Root}}"` **self-bindings** inside `FloorSidebarItem.xaml` (`:264`, `:273`, `:312`); one comment at `:259`.

**Not one binds either property to a view-model property — every binding sources `x:Reference Root`, the
control itself.** So `04-app-controls-and-theme.md:533` survives, and T2's phrasing is the precise reading:
*the mechanism changed; the ownership did not.* The page still owns the fact, but pushes it onto a legal
binding target rather than an imperative painter.

The reviewer also confirmed the **falsifications the survival claim sits beside**, so §1.7 is not made to
carry weight it should not: no declaration of `SetEditing`, `Deselect`, `SetExpanded`, `_isSelected` or
`_isExpanded` exists under `src/` — every hit is a comment recording the removal. And §1.9's staleness holds:
`sed -n '137,153p'` on `AreaSidebarItem.xaml.cs` lands in the `HoverEndedCommand`/`DeleteCommand` accessors,
`'185,221p'` on `FloorSidebarItem.xaml.cs` in the four `Area*Command` accessors — **no appearance code in
either range**, so the record's own `locations:` citations point at the wrong thing.
