## Advisory findings

Advisory findings carry no remediation options.

**A1 — `HasFlag(Control)` admits `Ctrl`+`Shift`+arrow and `Ctrl`+`Alt`+arrow.** `DungeonDetailPage.xaml.cs:390`.
C1 §2 says "no other modifier", but the criterion's words are about which key and modifier the branch *reads*,
and `HasFlag` is the exact form the contract cites as its proven shipped precedent (`Ctrl`+`0` at `:446`).
Tightening to `e.Modifiers == KeyboardModifiers.Control` would diverge from the file's one working precedent
for no measured gain. **Not a criterion violation.**

**A2 — the `_editor.SelectedFloorId` inconsistency is correct minimal scope, not a defect.** `:2271` versus
`:882`, `:2405`, `:2525`. The reviewer **ruled with T3**: C1 §4.2 freezes the `ExecuteAsync` call and is silent
on selection; no criterion requires it; and the three drop sites set it because *a pointer drag is itself an
act of pointing at that floor, which a keystroke on an already-focused handle is not.* Setting it would also
select the floor and refresh the map — an unrequested behaviour change — inside the same continuation that
must then restore focus. **Leave it out.** If keyboard reorder should later also select, it is the one-line
addition T3 described.

**A3 — T3's other three assumptions are all sound.** `_viewModel.FloorList.Items.Count` (`:2269`) is the right
count *precisely because* `FloorListContainer.Children` can hold the transient `_dragPlaceholder` `Border`
that `ShowDragPlaceholder` inserts (`:2446-2476`) — `Children.Count` would overcount by one mid-drag. Two
focused handles returning silently (`:2245`) matches C1 §3 and criterion 8's "exactly one". No
`HandCursorBehavior` is **not** a regression: the pre-change `Label`, read in the clean main checkout, carried
no behaviours either.

**A4 — `HorizontalTextAlignment`/`VerticalTextAlignment` were correctly dropped, and the glyph still centres.**
They come from `ITextAlignment`/`ITextAlignmentElement`, which `Label`, `Entry` and `EntryCell` implement and
`Button` does not — no `Button` member exists. Centring is the `Button` template's own content alignment, and
the repo's worked precedent is in this same file: `ExpandToggle` (`:123-144`), `NotebookButton` (`:176-194`)
and the delete button (`:197-214`) are all glyph-only `Button`s with no alignment attributes, and
`ExpandToggle`'s comment records its rendered glyph measured as matching them. Evidence: `external` + `repo`.

**A5 — the Appium clearance disclosure is sound and the file is correctly untouched.** A WinUI `Button`'s
control template applies its own measure and minimum sizing that `Padding`, `Margin` and `BorderWidth="0"` do
not pin, so `FloorDragHandleRightEdgeClearsTheScrollViewportRightEdgeByAtLeast14` can shift without anything
in this diff being wrong. That suite is outside `DungeonMaster.sln` and was never compiled by the solution
build, so it is **unverifiable here** and belongs with the §6 width observation.

**A6 — `DragHandle` alone among the file's five `Button`s omits `CornerRadius`.** `:264-279` versus `:135`,
`:188`, `:208`. Invisible while `BackgroundColor="Transparent"` and `BorderWidth="0"` — **but the WinUI focus
visual is the one thing that will now be drawn on this element**, and this is exactly the element that just
became focusable. Worth a glance during the observation pass. Not in C1's frozen table, so not a criterion
matter.

### A criterion defect — and it was the orchestrator's

**Round 1's criterion 9 predicted one failing test. Two fail.** The orchestrator authored amendment A1 §5A,
which changes the `Hint` string — and T4's `TheHandleAnnouncesTheKeyboardReorderRouteInItsHint` pins that

Passed: 794`, both in `FloorDragHandleTests`, none elsewhere. Recorded as a defective criterion, not a T3

### T3 caught an inert injection — its own

Proving the `CS0029` revert guard fires, T3's **first attempt was inert**: it changed only `ExpandToggle`'s
start tag, and `MAUIG1001` (mismatched end tag) plus a cascade of `CS0103` **masked `CS0029` entirely**. The
build failed loudly for reasons having nothing to do with the guard — which, read carelessly, is a successful
demonstration. T3 noticed the error codes were wrong, corrected the injection, re-ran, and only then claimed
the result.

**This is the failure the handoff catalogued nine times across two prior sessions, caught once.** Here it was
caught by the agent that made it, before anything was claimed.

**The reviewer reproduced the corrected injection independently and found more than T3 reported.** `CS0029`
fires at `:337` (`FocusReorderAffordance`) and `:385` (`ApplyExpanded`) with **no masking diagnostics** — and
critically, **removing `Clicked="OnExpandToggleClicked"` produced no diagnostic of its own.** Nothing else in
the build objects to reverting that element. **`CS0029` is the sole barrier** between a silent `MASTER-164`
regression and a shipped build, so the `Button`-typed local is load-bearing rather than decorative.

Scope limit the reviewer stated: the guard catches a **type** revert. It does not catch keeping the `Button`
and removing its focusability another way — `IsEnabled="False"`, or a tab-stop change. The comments claim
only what the guard does, so they are honest.
