## On the method — judged deliberately, not waved through

T3 created the three new files with `sed -n` into scratch (which strips CR) and then
`sed 's/$/\r/' scratch > target` to restore CRLF. The reviewer's finding: this is **within the letter
and the spirit** of criterion 7. The harness-preference suspension is scoped by the criterion to the
pre-existing file; `sed -i` was never used; and the criterion's permission for new-file creation is
conditioned on demonstrated CRLF, which is demonstrated and which the reviewer reproduced two ways.
**The theoretical loss channels — a bare CR, an absent trailing newline — were measured absent rather
than argued away.** `git status --porcelain` shows T3 touching only its four owned paths.

`public abstract` forms. Harmless here — the file's only public declaration is matched and both
private near-misses excluded — but the pattern must not be reused as an "any public type" census
elsewhere.
