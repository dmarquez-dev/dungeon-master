## Advisory findings

1. **`SetTextTrimmingOnChildren` execution semantics changed on the failure path.** It was previously
   skipped when any earlier statement threw; it now runs unconditionally after the catch, and its own
   exceptions propagate out of a `Loaded` handler instead of being swallowed. Both are *required* by
   criterion 1 and are the intended effect of making failure observable -- the tree walk is independent
   of the theme dictionary (it only sets `TextTrimming` on `TextBlock` children, lines 132-138), so
   running it after a failed dictionary attach is strictly better. Recorded so it is a decision rather
   than an accident.
2. **The new comment's WinUI claim is uncited.** Lines 100-102 assert "the WinUI resource system
   rejects a duplicate or ill-typed key rather than degrading." Evidence type: `judgment`, stated as
   belief -- the implementer raised this against itself and the reviewer did not spend a lookup, the
   budget being reserved for blocking findings. Safer worded as the reason the *filter* was chosen than
   as documented WinUI behaviour. Correctness does not depend on it; `COMException` is the standard
   failure mode for a WinRT interop call regardless.
3. **Fully-qualified `Microsoft.UI.Xaml.Media.SolidColorBrush` repeated seven times** (lines 74-80) is
   pre-existing style carried over unchanged by the move. Out of scope; noted only because the move
   makes it more visible.
