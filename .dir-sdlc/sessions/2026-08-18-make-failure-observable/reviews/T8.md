## The three judgement calls

**1. Criterion-1 repro substitution -- legitimate, not a dodge.** The guard exists exactly as reported:
`DungeonDetailPage.xaml.cs:617` reads
`var areaName = string.IsNullOrWhiteSpace(e.Name) ? lastGoodName : e.Name;` and `:621` returns without
dispatching when `areaName` is still blank. A cleared name on an existing area therefore dispatches the
**old** name -- a success -- and never reaches `.NotEmpty()`. The criterion's intent (a validator-rejected
write becomes visible and is not silently lost) is tested unchanged; only the keystrokes moved from
`.NotEmpty()` to `.MaximumLength(100)` **on the same rule chain of the same validator**.

**2. Before/after observation -- coherent with the code.** `parent02.png` (no message, sidebar repainted
to `Bar`) is exactly what the parent's unconditional `LoadDungeonAsync` produces. `shot09.png` (red row-1
label, typed name retained, content pushed down by the newly-occupied `Auto` row) is exactly what the new
early `return` plus the `IsVisible` converter produce. **Nothing in the reported evidence is inconsistent
with the readable code path.**

**3. Regression and fix -- both confirmed, arithmetic rechecked.** `controls:NotebookView` at line 641
sits at 8-space indent immediately before the outer grid's closing `</Grid>` (line 647), and its own
preceding comment says it spans "every row and column of the outermost grid" -- it is genuinely a
**fifth** top-level child. Contract §4's "exactly four top-level children" and its listing of line 633
among the nested eight are **both contract defects**. With five rows, `Grid.RowSpan="5"` is correct; at
`"4"` the notebook would have been confined to the four `Auto` rows and forced them to grow, matching the
reported ~225px jump. `final01.png` shows the notebook open with no jump. **Arithmetic: parent = 5 outer +
7 nested = 12 (matches `grep -c` on parent); after = 6 outer + 7 nested = 13 (matches).**

**Criterion 3's `AddAreaWithOutlineAsync` extraction -- satisfies, does not evade.** Both dispatches in the
flow clear `ErrorMessage`, capture `result`, and branch on `result.IsFailure` into the same channel; the
helper has one caller, carries an XML doc stating why it exists, and the parent's behaviour (no reload
after the outline write) is preserved exactly.

## Advisory findings

1. **T8's "mutually unsatisfiable" claim was slightly overstated.** A pair of sibling `{ ... }` blocks
   inside `AddAreaWithOutlineAsync` would have kept both dispatches lexically in the method with both
   locals named `result` and no CS0136. The extraction is the more idiomatic of the two -- noting only
   that the constraint did not strictly force it. (`DungeonDetailViewModel.cs:434`)
2. **`SaveNewAreaOutlineAsync` ends with a dead `return;`** inside the last `if` with nothing after it.
   Verbatim the frozen shape, so leaving it is defensible for grep-uniformity.
   (`DungeonDetailViewModel.cs:449-453`)
3. **Contract defect, already surfaced and correctly handled by T8**: `app-failure-surfacing.md` §4 states
   four top-level children and lists line 633 among eight nested `Grid.Row` attributes. Both wrong -- five
   top-level children and seven nested. **Worth correcting in the contract so Session 5 does not inherit
   the error.**
4. **`shot09.png` does not have the sidebar's area row in frame**, so the "not repainted" half of
   criterion 1(c) rests on the notebook retaining the typed name plus the code's early `return`. Sufficient,
   but a frame including the sidebar row would have made it self-evident.
