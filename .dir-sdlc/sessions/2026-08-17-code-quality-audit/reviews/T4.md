## The `.xaml.g.cs` exclusion — reviewer's ruling

**Defensible, and it does not understate coverage.** Three reasons: the contract's Scope section explicitly authorises excluding XAML source-generator output provided the document says which (it does, with reasons); `MauiXamlInflator=SourceGen` is genuinely set at `src/DungeonMaster.App/DungeonMaster.App.csproj:26`, so the claim is factual; and decisively, the generated partials land under `obj/`, not under `Controls/**` or `Theme/**`, so they fall outside criterion 7's own globs — the arithmetic would be numerically identical with or without the exclusion. `git ls-files` confirms zero `.g.cs` tracked.

Titles alone max at 95, and contract §5 says "*The title* … Maximum 100 characters", so the title-only reading is natural and this was not treated as a violation. Flagged in case T6's parser measures the whole heading line.
