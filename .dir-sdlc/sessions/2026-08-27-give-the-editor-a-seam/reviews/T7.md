2. **Criterion 12's `git status --porcelain` check is not usable as literally written in a shared worktree.** It names the owned files, but the worktree carries every concurrent task's edits, so a literal read fails for every task in the session. The boundary that actually held was criterion 8's named-file check plus the scoped diff. Tighten to `git status --porcelain -- <owns globs>` in future manifests. (Same finding as T1's advisory 4 — this is a method-level defect, not a per-task one.)

3. **The `CreateShell`/`Build` split is right and leaves no seam worth closing.** What drifted between the two palettes was the frame and the inset, and both now live in one place. The expander's tooltip-absence, its `SemanticProperties.SetHint`, its `OpenSecondaryPalette` tap target and its ellipsis label are genuinely its own; folding them into `Build` would mean parameterising four axes to share nothing. `AttachHover` is still called explicitly by the expander at `:308`, which a future reader could mistake for duplication — it is not, since `Build` calls it too and the expander is not built through `Build`.
