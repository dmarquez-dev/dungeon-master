## The reviewer's answers to the four questions put to it

1. **B11 holds independently.** The whole-file `Touch()` census is stronger than a read of five bodies:
   the five methods contain **zero** `Touch()` call sites, so the only route is the guarded one.
2. **Dispositions preserved, asymmetric pair included.** Not folding the guard into the helper is
   correct and is the only way to keep the two vocabularies separate under B4/B5. Reviewer agrees with
   T2's `backend.md` citation.
3. **Inner-absent paths are behaviour-preserving.** `TouchOnSuccess(floor.X(...))` evaluates the inner
   call first, then returns the argument unchanged when it is not `Success` — identical to the
   pre-refactor `return outcome;`. An unknown `areaId`/`objectId` still returns its outcome and still
   does not touch.
4. **Nothing in the public surface moved.** All ten multi-line parameter lists spot-checked, not a
   sample.
