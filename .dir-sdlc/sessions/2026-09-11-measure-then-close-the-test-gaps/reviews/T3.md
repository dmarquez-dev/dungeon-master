## Contradicts finding

**The frozen contract states a figure its own cited command does not produce, and T5 is scoped to restate it.**

`contracts/converter-behaviour.md:72-74` says: measured by `grep -rn "GreaterThanZeroConverter" src/DungeonMaster.App/` — **7 lines**.

Verified by the orchestrator in the worktree, all four variants:

| Command | Result |
|---|---|
| bare, as the contract cites it | **15** |
| `--exclude-dir=obj` | 10 |
| `--exclude-dir=obj --exclude-dir=bin` | 8 |
| `--include=*.xaml --exclude-dir=obj --exclude-dir=bin` | **7** |

What is true instead: there are **7 XAML sites** (1 resource declaration at `App.xaml:21` + 6 bindings), of
which **2** pass `ConverterParameter=Invert` — `DungeonDetailPage.xaml:151` and `DungeonListPage.xaml:115`.
That substance is correct; only the grep-line framing is false, and **its value swings with build-artifact
state**, so no line count is stable. The implementer's own advisory under-diagnosed this: it reported 8 and
proposed `--exclude-dir=obj`, which yields 10, not 8.

### Ranked remediation options

1. **T5 states "7 XAML sites in `src/DungeonMaster.App/`, 2 with `ConverterParameter=Invert`" and cites the
   file:line list rather than any count-producing command.** The enumerated sites are stable under build
   state; a grep-line count is not. *Evidence: repo — the seven paths and lines, and the two `Invert` sites
   enumerated in the contract at `:77-78`.*
2. **Keep a command but scope it to XAML and exclude build output:**
   `grep -rn "GreaterThanZeroConverter" src/DungeonMaster.App/ --include=*.xaml --exclude-dir=obj --exclude-dir=bin` → 7.
   Preserves a reproducible command at the cost of a longer, more fragile invocation. *Evidence: repo — the
   15/10/8/7 progression above.*
3. **Amend the contract line itself before T5 runs.** Cleanest at the source, but the contract froze at
   Gate 2 and amending it is an orchestrator decision, not a task's. *Evidence: judgment.*

**Orchestrator disposition:** option 1 adopted, and T5's dispatch carries it explicitly. The contract line is
left as frozen; this review file is the record of why T5's wording departs from it.
