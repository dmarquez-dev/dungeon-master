evidence `repo`; (2) fix the two wrong counts and replace the bare figures with prose carrying no number,
since C13 binds counts — evidence `repo`; (3) fix only the two false counts — evidence `judgment`, ranked last
because it leaves C13's universal unmet on its own terms. **Option 1 was taken.**
