The orchestrator had framed this as "the restated-claim pattern in miniature." **That was a misdiagnosis, and the reviewer's is better.** 1353 = 1352 + 1 and 1227 = 1226 + 1 is exactly what `content.split('\n')` yields on a newline-terminated file — the phantom trailing element.
