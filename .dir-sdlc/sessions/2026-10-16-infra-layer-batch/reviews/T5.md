## Instrument hazard — a THIRD broken escaped-pipe counter, in a third tool

Counting escaped pipes in awk via `index($0, "\\|")` **silently returns 0 for every row**: awk warns
`escape sequence \| treated as plain |`, so the needle becomes `|`, escaped equals total, and unescaped
computes as **0**. The reviewer's first census reported "unescaped=0 rows=181" with complete confidence.
Building the needle as `sprintf("%c%c", 92, 124)` fixes it, validated on a fixture where a row with one `\|`
reported 7 total / 1 escaped / 6 unescaped and a clean row reported 7 unescaped.

**That is three broken escaped-pipe counters in this session, in three tools, by three parties** — the
orchestrator's awk `gsub` and Python regex, T5's `awk -F` and `gsub`, and this one. The task is counting two
characters.
