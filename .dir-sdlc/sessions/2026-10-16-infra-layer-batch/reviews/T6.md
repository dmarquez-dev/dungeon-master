## Advisory findings

**A1 — criterion 3's statement that the opener is "closed by `**]**`" is FALSE, and T6 was right to follow
the measured precedent instead.** `grep -c -F '**]**'` on the precedent file returns **0**; the ten `6b-xix`
blocks close with a plain `]` appended to the final sentence. T6's six blocks each close
`…added by the orchestrator at Gate 3.]`, matching the precedent exactly.

As the reviewer put it: *"The `**]**` phrase is a false detail in the frozen text, not a divergence between
a criterion's letter and its purpose — the criterion's own governing instruction is to take the form from the
arc's existing blocks, which is what T6 did."*

**It is explicitly NOT a `contradicts` finding:** `grep -rn -F '**]**'` across the criteria files returns
only T6's own, **so no other scoped task is moved by the falsification.** Worth repairing in the manifest
text for the next session of the arc so the gloss does not propagate.

**The orchestrator carries responsibility for this one.** The `**]**` detail originated in the Gate 2
manifest and was then *repeated verbatim in the T6 dispatch prompt*, after the orchestrator had already
measured the precedent's bracket form and still mis-stated its closer.

**A2 — two of the nine corrections carry their verbatim quote without the stock `Previous wording,
verbatim:` phrase.** At `:501` (quoting the superseded predicate and its count of 10) and `:1044` (quoting
`"no code reference changes"`). **Both do quote the prior claim verbatim, so the auditability the criterion
calls load-bearing is intact** — only the phrase that makes the quotes greppable is missing, so
