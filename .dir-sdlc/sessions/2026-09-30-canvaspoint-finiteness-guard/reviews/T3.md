## The guard, and why its shape is the finding

```csharp
private readonly float x = Finite(X, nameof(X));
public float X { get => x; init => x = Finite(value, nameof(X)); }
```

`float.IsFinite` is the entire predicate. **Two mechanisms guard two different paths**, and the review
established that neither is redundant:

| Path | Guarded by | Proven by |
|---|---|---|
| primary constructor | the private field initializers | **Injection C** — the reviewer's own |
| `with`-expression | the `init` accessors | **Injection B** — T3's, reproduced |

**T3 discovered the field initializers are load-bearing by hitting `error CS8907` ("Parameter 'X' is
unread") twice**: declaring `X`/`Y` explicitly suppresses the compiler's positional assignment, so without
the initializers the parameters are dropped and every point reads back `0`.

|---|---|---|---|
| **B** (T3's, reproduced by the reviewer) | both `init` bodies -> `init => x = value;`, field initializers kept — i.e. **the `PathSegment` initializer-only shape** | `Failed: 6, Passed: 13, Total: 19`; the six failures **exactly** the three `With_SettingXNonFinite_*` and three `With_SettingYNonFinite_*`, every constructor case green | the `init` accessor is the **sole** guard on the `with` path |
| **C** (the reviewer's own, T3 did not run it) | field initializers -> `= X` / `= Y`, `init` guards kept | `Failed: 8, Passed: 11, Total: 19`; the eight failures **exactly** the constructor-path cases, all six `with` cases green | the field initializers are the **sole** guard on the constructor path |

> **Injection B is why criterion 5 existed.** It reproduces the carried `MASTER-170` bypass: a record's
> compiler-generated copy constructor assigns backing fields directly and never re-runs a property
> initializer, so the shape `PathSegment.cs:35` uses guards the constructor and leaves `with` open — which is

> not repeat it, and the injection is the proof rather than the claim.**

`Expected System.ArgumentOutOfRangeException, but no exception was thrown`. The 5 passing were the

## The orchestrator's error, and how it bears on this review

T3's dispatch told it *"no existing call site constructs a non-finite `CanvasPoint`, and your guard should
break nothing"*, citing 78 sites / 5 production / **0** non-finite. **That claim was false, and the reason is
a predicate error rather than a stale anchor.** T1 supplies its non-finite values as xUnit `[InlineData]`
theory parameters, so the construction line reads `new CanvasPoint(x, y)` with no `NaN` or `Infinity` token
on it; the filter `grep "new CanvasPoint(" | grep -E "NaN|Infinity"` **can never see those rows**, and
re-running it today still returns only hits in T3's own new file.

**T3 diagnosed both defects unprompted** — the stale anchor and, more importantly, the predicate blindness —
and counted a second way: `grep "InlineData" | grep -cE "NaN|Infinity"` returns **24** rows, **7** of them
T1's at `:27-29` and `:63-66`, matching the 7 failures exactly. The reviewer was told not to hold against T3
a consequence the orchestrator's own bad census said would not happen, and did not.
