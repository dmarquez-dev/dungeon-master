## Advisory findings

Advisories carry **no remediation options** — the reviewer attaches options to blocking findings only.

**A1. `NonFiniteScaleRejectionTests.cs:20` names the wrong comparer type.** It says `InclusiveBetween`
compares through `Comparer<float>.Default`. It compares through
`FluentValidation.Validators.ComparableComparer<float>.Instance`, established by loading the shipped DLL and
invoking it. The two coincide numerically for `float`, so **the wording is undetectable from the verdict**,
which is why it would survive uncorrected. **T5 must not carry the `Comparer<float>.Default` wording into
the register.**

**A2. `NonFiniteScaleRejectionTests.cs:19`'s inference is unqualified and form-dependent.** Its two cited
sub-facts are true (`NaN < Min` and `NaN > Max` both `false`, both confirmed). The inference *"so an
operator-based range check would ACCEPT `NaN`"* holds only for the **negative** form
`if (v < from || v > to) fail`. The equally natural **positive** form `if (!(v >= from && v <= to)) fail`
**rejects** `NaN`, because `NaN >= Min` is `false`. As written it is an unqualified universal over
"operator-based range check" that is false for one of the two standard formulations.

> **This is also the explanation for a divergence recorded earlier in the session.** `measurement.md` §2(e)
> reasoned that `InclusiveBetween` rejects `NaN` "because every ordered comparison against `NaN` is false";
> T2 reported that reasoning would have produced "the opposite verdict". **Both were reasoning from
> different implicit forms, and neither said which.** The orchestrator's conclusion happened to be right
> for the positive form; T2's correction of it was right for the negative form and overbroad as stated. The
> reviewer's measurement is what settles it, and §2(e) is corrected accordingly.

**A3. The `MathClamp` test's literals are uncoupled from their source.** `0.25f, 4.0f` are duplicated from
`DungeonDetailPage.xaml.cs:500` and would not notice an edit there. **Important:** those zoom bounds
coincidentally equal `ObjectScale.Min`/`Max` (`ObjectScale.cs:5-6`) while being an unrelated concept, so
**do not "fix" this by referencing `ObjectScale`.** Either hoist the zoom bounds to a named constant both
sites read, or leave it with the doc comment's existing caveat, which is already honest.

**A4. Naming form is mixed within the file, trivially.** Nine tests use `Subject_WithCondition_Outcome`;
`MathClampWithNaNReturnsNaNRatherThanABound` drops the underscores. **Not a deviation from the
neighbourhood** — `FloorMapDrawableExecutionTests.cs:35` is `ScreenToCanvasInvertsPanAndZoom`, the same
underscore-free form. Both forms exist in the project and the cited rule mandates neither.

**A5. T2's out-of-criteria finding is confirmed and should be carried forward.** See "The scale path is
closed; the position path is not" above.
