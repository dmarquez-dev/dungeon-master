## The large diff, checked rather than accepted

T3 claimed the 656/439 diff is mostly re-indentation from wrapping five method bodies in `try`.
**Verified:** `git diff -w --stat` against the same base gives **245 insertions / 28 deletions**.

The substantive change set is exactly: 5 guards wrapping existing bodies, 9 new guarded
lambdas/handlers, 12 `_ =` → `await`, 3 signature conversions to `async Task`, 5 handler conversions to
`async void`, one new `LoadFailed` event plus subscriber, two `PreloadObjectImagesAsync` call sites,
and one `static` removal. **No unintended behavioural change hides in it.**

## The four judgment calls

**1. The 19 count holds, and the four extra units are forced rather than chosen.** Criterion 3's rule
is "*every* async void unit in both owned files is guarded"; its enumeration is illustrative and its
middle term carries no number. The decomposition: 5 named + 6 existing lambdas + 2 contract-created +
2 G3-forced (`OnMapStartInteraction`, `OnMapDoubleTapped`) + 4 extra. **Each of the four held a discard
in the base blob** — `OnKeyDown` at 351/369/388, `OnMapSecondaryTapped` at 1662, the two
`ControlsPalette` lambdas at 143 and 163 — and criterion 5 bans the discard while criterion 2's G3 rule
bans a non-`On*` `async void` wrapper, so the await had to propagate into the enclosing handler. This
**satisfies** criterion 3; it does not exceed it.

**2. Dropping `static` from `AttachPopupButtonBehavior` is in scope.** Criterion 3 makes
`tap.Tapped += async` (`:2029`) a G1 unit; criterion 4 fixes its error channel as
`_viewModel.ErrorMessage`; a `static` method cannot reach an instance field. The change is private,
alters no public surface, and every call site is inside the constructor. **Not a finding.**

**3. "Not exercisable in this tree" is the precise characterisation, and the criterion is the defect.**
`ObjectPackRegistry.AvailablePacks` is a one-element collection literal at `ObjectPackRegistry.cs:8-9`,
and `OnPackPickerChanged` returns without work when `SelectedIndex` is out of range — re-selecting
index 0 raises no `PackChanged`. Criterion 8 is recorded not met because it is written as a conjunction
and one conjunct was not observed, **but the unmet half is a criterion defect, not a work defect.**

**4. The preload inference holds, and it is the session's strongest runtime evidence.**
`grep -rn "_imageCache" --include=*.cs src/DungeonMaster.App` returns nine hits, of which exactly three
touch the dictionary's contents: `FloorMapDrawable.cs:51` (`Clear()`), `:76`
(`_imageCache[kind] = PlatformImage.FromStream(stream)`) and `:84` (`= null` on failure). **Both
writers are inside `PreloadObjectImagesAsync`.** `LoadOrGetImage` at `:352-353` is an expression-bodied
`TryGetValue`. So a non-null cache entry can only have been produced by line 76; real artwork on screen
entails line 76 ran; and the only two invocations of that method are T3's own new call sites.
