## Round 1's blocking finding, and what happened to it

**B1 had two halves. One was wrong; one was right.**

**The wrong half — do not let it re-enter the record.** B1 claimed the tally sentence should read
58 / one / two rather than 59 / one / one, on the basis that `grep -ci "NOT MET"` over the six review
tables returns 3. **That count came from a stale artifact of the orchestrator's own making:** the third
hit was `reviews/T4.md`, still carrying criterion 6 as unmet because it was written *before* the user
performed the manual hover and was not updated after escalation E4 resolved. T4 criterion 6 **is met**.
`reviews/T4.md` was corrected, with its round-1 verdict preserved above the resolution.

The lesson is in the grep, not the number: **an evidence cell can contain the words "NOT MET" while
recording a met verdict.** Matching on the verdict column — `grep -cE "^\| [0-9]+ \|[^|]*\|[^|]*NOT MET"` —
is what distinguishes the two, and round 2 re-derived the tally that way and confirmed T7 was right.

**The right half.** `MASTER-019` carried a bare `**done**` with no note that two of its four
`DungeonListPage` guards were never observed running, so escalation **E5 left no trace in the durable
record** while E2/E3, E4 and E6 each did. That asymmetry is exactly the rot the qualification
convention exists to prevent.
