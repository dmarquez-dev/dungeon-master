## Advisory findings

**A1 — The leading blank line removed from `src/DungeonMaster.sln:1` is a third edit, where contract §5 says "two changes, and nothing else."**
Reviewer's wording: the file began `ef bb bf 0d 0a 4d 69 63`; §5b authorises stripping only `ef bb bf`, which would have left `0d 0a 4d` and failed criterion 4's demand for `4d 69 63`. The contract and the criterion are not jointly satisfiable, the implementer took the criterion, and disclosed the choice rather than burying it. Advisory rather than blocking on three grounds: criterion 4 is the narrower and byte-exact instruction and is the standard the reviewer judges against; the removed byte pair carries no meaning to MSBuild's `.sln` parser, which skips leading blank lines before the format header, so no build behaviour changes — corroborated by the 0-warning build and 292 passing tests; and the only contract-literal alternative was to fail an acceptance criterion, so a revision round could not improve the outcome. **This is a defect in the contract's byte accounting, not in the implementation.** Worth carrying into the Gate 3 record so §5's "two changes" wording is not later read as evidence of drift.
