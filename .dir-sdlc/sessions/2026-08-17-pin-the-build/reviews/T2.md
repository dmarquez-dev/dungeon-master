## Blocking findings

**B1 — the `IDE0055` backlog count is wrong, and the error is exactly the thing that would have broken T3.**

Violates criterion 9 and contract `editorconfig.md` §4 ("the count is what tells Session 7 how big the item is"). `.editorconfig:64` read `# formatting; 58 space-indented files`. Measured, `IDE0055` fires on **62 distinct files and ~14,160 locations**, and the set is not the one the comment names.

- **The migrations are exempt.** All five files under `Migrations/` are 100% space-indented, and **zero** `IDE0055` diagnostics were reported in any of them. `generated_code = true` works as the contract intends; the hypothesis put to the reviewer was disproved.
- **But five files T3 would never touch do fire.** Each is already 100% tab-indented, so absent from T3's list by §6's old definition, yet each carries `IDE0055` violations of a different kind — intra-line column alignment: `DungeonDetailPage.xaml.cs` (68 locations), `ControlsPaletteView.xaml.cs` (3), `PlacedObject.cs` (4), `DungeonReadRepository.cs` (3), `HandCursorBehavior.cs` (1).

**Count reconciliation.** There is no 58th file. Contract §6 enumerated **57** paths (App 7, Application 1, Domain 4, Infrastructure 1, Tests 44 — over a sub-header claiming 45). "58" was an arithmetic error. The 62 in the space-indent census = those 57 plus the 5 migrations. The 62 that `IDE0055` reports = those same 57 plus the 5 alignment-only files, and no migrations. Two different 62s that happened to coincide, which is what made the numbers look reconcilable.

**Ranked remediation options, as the reviewer worded them:**

1. **Widen T3 to the empirical `IDE0055` set and let `dotnet format whitespace` do all 62 files.** §7 already sanctions that tool, and it fixes alignment as well as indentation, so the commit stays whitespace-only and semantically inert — the property T3 exists to protect is preserved. Requires unfreezing §6's list (57 to 62) and relaxing §7's "leading whitespace only" to "whitespace only". Also correct `.editorconfig:64`'s comment to `62 files`. *Evidence: repo.*
2. **Correct the count now, and defer the `IDE0055` flip to Session 7.** T3 ships the reformat and leaves the line at `suggestion` alongside the other twelve backlog rules. Smallest change and no unfreezing beyond deleting §5, but it forfeits this session's proof that `.editorconfig` is enforced rather than advisory. *Evidence: repo — §5 states that proof is the point of the flip.*
3. **Keep T3's list frozen and add a path-scoped `suggestion` override for the five files.** Works, but contradicts §3 ("no `dotnet_diagnostic` line outside the block") and leaves five permanently-exempt files with no backlog entry — the opposite of "legible as backlog". *Evidence: judgment.*

**Resolution:** escalated to the user (escalation `E3`), who chose **option 1**. Contract sections 5 through 8 and the manifest's T3 entry were amended accordingly under that authority; T2 was re-dispatched for one revision round to correct the count comment.

## Round-2 finding B2 — raised blocking, resolved as partly incorrect

The reviewer raised **B2** against the orchestrator's §§5-8 amendment (not against T2's deliverable), claiming the widened T3 scope was unachievable on three grounds. The orchestrator verified each directly before acting. **One premise is false; two are correct.**

| B2 premise | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `dotnet format whitespace` will convert LF to CRLF across 31 files, and `.editorconfig`'s `end_of_line = crlf` is "exactly backwards" against a tree that is 178/178 LF | **FALSE** | Byte-level count over every `.cs` file under `src/`: **178 CRLF-only, 0 LF-only, 0 mixed**. `git ls-files --eol` independently reports `i/lf w/crlf`. Every replacement string in the dry-run report already emits `\r\n`. The reviewer's LF finding came from a `grep $'\r'` under MSYS, which normalises CR out of text-mode reads; the "453 newline-rewriting changes" were ordinary whitespace fixes whose replacement text contains `\r\n`. `end_of_line = crlf` matches the tree, and §7's "no line-ending change" rule is satisfiable as written. |
| It strips byte-order marks, which §7 forbids | **TRUE** | 5 files carry `Fix file encoding`; 10 `.cs` files hold a BOM in total. Resolved by amending §7: BOM *removal* is permitted and expected, because `repository-root-config.md` §6's `utf-8`, no-BOM decision is already binding on T3 and the "no BOM change" bullet contradicted it. |
| It touches files outside T3's 62 | **TRUE** | Exactly **2**: `Platforms/MacCatalyst/AppDelegate.cs` and `Platforms/MacCatalyst/Program.cs`, whose only change is the BOM strip. Resolved by amending §7 to require `--include` scoping to the 62 paths. |

**What the same report also proved, which is the answer to the question B2 was raised against:** the formatter's footprint covers **all 62 of T3's files, misses none, and touches zero files under `Migrations/`**. The widened scope is achievable.
