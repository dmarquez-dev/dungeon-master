## The reviewer rejected the task's own proof and replaced it

This is the most substantive thing in the review, and the verdict rests on the replacement.

**The conservation argument as T5 stated it does not hold.** Its four checks -- a reverse-transform
census reproducing `46/29/18/18/6`, `wc -l` equality, a signature dump, a mangle scan -- are each
necessary and none sufficient. A census is a **count-level** check and counts are not injective:
reproducing those five figures exactly is consistent with any count-neutral edit, such as a `ct` moved
between two parameter positions in the same signature, two arguments transposed at a call site, or a
parameter reordered. `wc -l` equality excludes insertion and deletion but not substitution within a line.
The signature dump covers only `Common/Dispatch` and `Common/Interfaces`, leaving the other 26 files on
the census alone.

**The replacement is a byte-identity proof.** The reviewer first measured the premise rather than
assuming it: `git grep -c -w 'cancellationToken' 00a093a` over both projects returns **no hits**, so a
blanket reverse map is unambiguous. It then applied `sed 's/\bcancellationToken\b/ct/g'` **to stdout**
over every T5-owned file in the diff and compared `md5sum` against `git show 00a093a:<path>`. **33 of 34
files are byte-identical to their `00a093a` content after the reverse rename.** Byte identity is
injective -- it leaves no room for a reordering, a transposition, a whitespace change or a line-ending
change. The 34th, `FloorObjectMethods.cs`, is proven by reading: its diff is 3 hunks totalling 9 changed
pairs, matching its `--stat` figure of 18, so the diff read is the complete diff and contains nothing but
the three local renames.

The two files that came back `DIFFERS` are exactly `PlacedObjectDto.cs` and `AreaConfiguration.cs` --
T3's and T4's, outside T5's `owns`. That is the expected result and corroborates no ownership violation.

**Orchestrator verification of the replacement:** `git diff --stat 00a093a` over the 10 `owns` paths
returns **34 files changed, 126 insertions, 126 deletions** -- symmetric, independently confirming no line
added or removed. Baseline `cancellationToken` at `00a093a` is **0**, whole-word `ct` at `00a093a` is
**117**, and `cancellationToken` now is **117**: exact token conservation. The md5 method reproduces on
spot-check against `IQueryHandler.cs`.

**On the 34-file census:** sound but unnecessary, and the reviewer did not rely on it. A diff was
available the whole time and is the better instrument, since a content census would silently miscount any
file edited in a way that removed the token rather than adding it.
