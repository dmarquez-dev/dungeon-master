Its differ strips **all** whitespace, so it is blind to a word-boundary-only rewording. The reviewer
demonstrated this: injecting `"The scale"` -> `"Thescale"` into a scratch copy, the implementer's fold

instrument critique, not a finding against the work. The general shape is the one this project keeps
