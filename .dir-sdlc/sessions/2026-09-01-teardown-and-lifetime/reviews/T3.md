## Advisory findings

1. **`StartingWhileRunningRestartsSoOnlyTheLastStartCanElapse` cannot observe restart itself.** Against the frozen fake, the second `Start` is a no-op on `IsRunning`, so the test is behaviourally indistinguishable from `ASingleStartRaisesElapsedOnceHoweverManyTimesTheTimerIsTicked`. That the earlier deadline is *cancelled* became unobservable through the §3 surface once `LastDelay`/`StartCount` were removed at Gate 2. The criterion asks only for the named test and the "only the last `Start` can elapse" outcome, both present and non-vacuous — **recorded so the gap is visible, not to reopen the Gate 2 decision.**
2. **`ANullDispatcherThrowsArgumentNullExceptionNamingDispatcher` sits in the host-collection class** although its guard runs before any MAUI type is touched, so it serialises against the host fixture for no reason. The implementer flagged this itself; cost is negligible at this suite size.
3. **`Start` sets `_timer.Interval = delay` with no floor, and `TimeSpan.Zero` is accepted.** No consumer passes zero and the contract rejects only negatives, but the behaviour of a zero WinUI dispatcher interval is unmeasured. Worth a look if a future consumer ever passes a computed delay.

## Note for the record

Advisory 1 is a direct consequence of a Gate 2 decision — trimming `LastDelay`/`StartCount`/`StopCount` from the contract for having no consumer removed the only surface through which restart cancellation was observable. The trim was still right on the evidence available at Gate 2; this is the cost, recorded.
