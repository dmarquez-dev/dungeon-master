## Contradicts

### C1 — Criterion 6 names a type that does not exist

`manifest.json`, T4 acceptance criterion 6, requires casting to `Microsoft.Maui.Controls.Internals.IPanGestureController`. Reflection over the built `Microsoft.Maui.Controls.dll` shows the interface is public at `Microsoft.Maui.Controls.IPanGestureController`, and that **no `Internals` type matching `*PanGesture*` exists in that assembly**. The criterion's escalation trigger was written for unreachability, which is not what is wrong, so continuing was the right call — but the criterion as written is unsatisfiable and would fail a literal re-check.

| Rank | Option | Rationale | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Amend the criterion text to `Microsoft.Maui.Controls.IPanGestureController` and record the correction in the session outcome | The type is public, reachable, and already used; only the manifest's namespace is wrong, so this is a text fix with no work attached | `repo` — reflection over `Microsoft.Maui.Controls.dll`, plus the test assembly compiling against the unqualified name under implicit usings |
| 2 | Leave the criterion verbatim and note the divergence in the session record only | Preserves the manifest as an unedited historical artifact, at the cost of a criterion that reads as unmet to anyone re-checking it literally | `judgment` |

### C2 — Criterion 7's stated injection does not discriminate the guard it targets

`manifest.json`, T4 acceptance criterion 7, says making `OnPanUpdated` skip clearing `_touching` shows "the released-press case" able to fail. But the case criterion 6 mandates — `APressReleasedBeforeTheDelayElapsesRaisesNothing` — **still passes under that injection**: the same arm calls `_longPressTimer?.Stop()`, `FakeLongPressTimer.Stop()` clears `IsRunning`, and `Tick()` returns early before ever reaching the guard. Reviewer reproduced this: `Passed: 6` of 8, with only the two re-armed-tick tests red. The added tests re-arm the fake after the release so the tick is delivered, isolating `_touching` as the only thing that can suppress the raise. **That demonstration is sound, not vacuous.**

| Rank | Option | Rationale | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Amend criterion 7 to name a re-armed-tick test as the discrimination target rather than the criterion-6 case | That is the only shape in which the injection discriminates, and it is the shape already shipped, so the amendment records what was actually proved | `repo` — reproduced injection run quoted under criterion 7 |
| 2 | Additionally amend criterion 6's released-press case to assert the guard, not only `Stop()` | Would keep the mandated three cases individually meaningful, but duplicates what the two added tests already cover | `judgment` |
| 3 | Leave both criteria and record the finding in the session outcome | Cheapest, but leaves a criterion whose stated proof is known not to prove its clause | `judgment` |
